ClipRift Studio

ClipRift Studio is a personal desktop tool for creators. It lets you upload your own short-form videos to your own TikTok and Instagram accounts from one place on your own computer, sending them to your account's inbox or drafts so you can review each video before it is posted.

The tool runs locally on the user's own machine and connects only to accounts the user owns and signs in to. It is a personal project, provided as-is.
