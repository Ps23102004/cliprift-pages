ClipRift Studio

ClipRift Studio is a personal desktop tool for creators. It lets you upload your own short-form videos to your own YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram accounts from one place on your own computer, sending them to your account's inbox or drafts so you can review each video before it is posted.

Videos posted to YouTube go to your own YouTube channel, with the title and privacy setting you choose. Use of the YouTube features is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the app's use of YouTube API Services is described in the Privacy Policy below.

The tool runs locally on the user's own machine and connects only to accounts the user owns and signs in to. It is a personal project, provided as-is.
