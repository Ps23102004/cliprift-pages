Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

ClipRift Studio is a desktop application that runs entirely on the user's own computer. It has no servers of its own and does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

YouTube API Services

ClipRift Studio uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to your own YouTube channel, to confirm which channel you are signed in to, and to read statistics of public videos. By using ClipRift Studio with YouTube, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses through the YouTube API: your channel's ID and name (to make sure uploads go to the right channel), the video files you choose to upload together with the title and privacy setting you set, and view statistics of videos. The app requests the OAuth scopes youtube.upload, youtube.readonly, and YouTube Analytics read-only access, and nothing else.

Where your data is stored: the OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only in your operating system's keychain (for example, macOS Keychain) on your own computer. No tokens, videos, or personal data are ever sent to or stored on a server run by the developer.

Revoking access and deleting data: you can revoke ClipRift Studio's access at any time on your Google Account permissions page, myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's keychain entries deletes the stored tokens, and removing the app removes everything it stored locally. For any data question or deletion request, contact devilucifer847@gmail.com.

How it works

When you connect a YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram account, you sign in directly with that platform. The access token the platform returns is stored only in your operating system's keychain on your own computer, under your control. Videos you choose are uploaded from your computer directly to the platform using that platform's official API.

Data we collect

None. The tool includes no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party data sharing.

Your control

You can disconnect an account at any time by deleting the tool's keychain entries or revoking the app's access in the platform's settings.
