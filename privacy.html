Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

ClipRift Studio is a desktop application that runs entirely on the user's own computer. It has no servers of its own and does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

How it works

When you connect a TikTok or Instagram account, you sign in directly with that platform. The access token the platform returns is stored only in a local configuration file on your own computer, under your control. Videos you choose are uploaded from your computer directly to the platform using that platform's official API.

Data we collect

None. The tool includes no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party data sharing.

Your control

You can disconnect an account at any time by deleting the tool's local configuration file or revoking the app's access in the platform's settings.
